How it works

From problem to parts list to done

01

Post your problem

Snap a photo, describe what's wrong. AI helps you name the parts and ask a clear question.

02

Get answers from people

Neighbors, hobbyists and tradespeople who've fixed the same thing reply and vote up what works.

03

Walk in with a list

Every solved thread ends with a parts and tools list you can take straight to the store.

Safety first. Some jobs — gas lines, main electrical panels, structural changes — belong to licensed professionals. HandyHuman will flag those clearly instead of pretending everything is DIY.
